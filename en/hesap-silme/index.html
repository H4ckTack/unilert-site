Delete your account and data · Unilert

You can delete your Unilert account and all associated data in two ways:

In the app (immediately)

  1. Open Unilert and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete my account at the bottom.
  3. Confirm with Delete permanently.

By email

If you can't access the app, email info@unilert.app from the address registered to your account with the subject "Account deletion". Requests are processed within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your account (email, user ID), follows, device notification registrations, subscription status record and rewarded ad records are deleted from our servers immediately and from backups within 30 days.

Note: deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.
