UnilertBot · Unilert

UnilertBot reads public announcement pages of universities for the Unilert app.

  • It obeys robots.txt.
  • It sends at most one request every few seconds per site and uses conditional requests.
  • It only reads announcement listing pages and never logs in or submits forms.
  • Only the title, a short excerpt, the date and the link are stored; readers are always sent to your site.

To limit access add a User-agent: UnilertBot rule to robots.txt or write to info@unilert.app.
