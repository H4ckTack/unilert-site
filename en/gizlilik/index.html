Privacy Policy · Unilert

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains which personal data the Unilert mobile app ("App") processes, why and how. The data controller is Zekeriya Hekimhan (contact: info@unilert.app).

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: email address, Firebase user ID (UID) and, if you sign in with Google, your Google display name. Your password never reaches our servers; it is stored hashed by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Preferences: the universities, faculties and departments you follow; app language.
  • Notification data: your Firebase Cloud Messaging registration token and platform (Android/iOS).
  • Subscription status: your plan and its expiry date. Payments are processed by Google Play / the App Store; we never receive payment or card details. Subscriptions are verified through RevenueCat.
  • Rewarded ad records: transaction IDs and timestamps of rewarded ads you watched, signed by Google AdMob (to compute the weekly reward).
  • Advertising data: on the Free and Pro plans ads are served by Google AdMob, which processes data such as the advertising ID and device information under its own policies. You are asked for consent to personalised ads on first launch and can change it under "Ad privacy choices". VIP has no ads.
  • Technical logs: IP address and time of requests, kept briefly for security and abuse prevention.

Saved announcements are stored only on your device.

2. Why we process data

  • to create your account and show announcements from the units you choose (performance of contract),
  • to send notifications and the daily summary,
  • to verify subscriptions and rewards,
  • to show ads so the app can stay free (consent for personalised ads),
  • to keep the service secure (legitimate interest).

3. Recipients

Data is shared only with providers needed to run the service: Google LLC (Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Messaging, AdMob, Google Play), RevenueCat Inc. and our server provider in Istanbul. Google and RevenueCat operate servers outside Türkiye; transfers are made under Article 9 of Law No. 6698 (KVKK). We do not sell your data.

4. Announcement content

Announcements are collected automatically from public websites of universities and public institutions. Only the title, a short excerpt, the date and the original link are shown; documents or lists that may contain names or student numbers are not copied.

5. Retention

Data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, preferences, devices and reward records are deleted from our servers immediately and from backups within 30 days.

6. Security

All connections use HTTPS. The server only accepts sessions signed by Google, and plans and rewards are verified directly with Google/RevenueCat/AdMob rather than trusted from the device.

7. Your rights

To exercise your rights under Article 11 of KVKK (access, rectification, erasure, objection, etc.) write to info@unilert.app. You can also delete your account in the app: Delete account.

8. Age

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over.

9. Changes

Updates are published on this page; significant changes are announced in the App.
